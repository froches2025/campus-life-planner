About

Campus Life Planner is a task management app built for students who want a simple way to keep track of everything they have going on. Beyond assignments and deadlines, it covers the full range of student life: meals, gym sessions, club meetings, personal errands, and anything else that takes up your time. The goal is to give you a clear picture of your week so you can plan more intentionally and avoid the feeling of constantly playing catch-up.

The app lets you log tasks with a title, due date, duration, and tag, then search and sort them however you need. The dashboard summarises your workload at a glance, including a 7-day activity trend and a weekly hour cap you can set yourself. Everything is saved locally in your browser, so your data stays on your device.
